Conclusion

Hypothesis REJECTED. PR #4577 did NOT cause the daily benchmark regression. In fact, B (post-#4577) is 5–9% FASTER than A (pre-#4577) on all six point-query workloads, with p<0.001 (Mann-Whitney, two-sided). The 5–9% improvement is consistent across all workload types (read, write, edge-write, index scan, property scan, create). The regression must originate from a different PR in the merge window: #4904, #4903, #4879, or a combination thereof.

The 5–9% improvement from #4577 may actually be masking the regression — if #4577 improved performance by ~7% and the CI shows only 2–3% net regression, the true regression delta from the other PRs may be ~9–10%.
